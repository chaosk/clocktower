# How to use the app

## First steps

<Callout type="warning">
  We've been seeing performance problems with Safari, consider using a different browser.
</Callout>

1. Sign up to the [Blood on the Clocktower app](https://botc.app). It's free.
2. Log in.
3. Allow for camera/microphone access.
4. Press `[Q]` to open settings → go to **Chat**.
5. Select your microphone/camera device from the respective dropdowns (note that they look like headers).

Treat the Grimoire as your personal notebook. Use it to track:

- Info you receive
- Characters others share with you
- Any theories or observations

## Private conversations

### At night

During the night phase, you can only privately send text messages your neighbors — simulating whispered conversations.

Press `[Enter]` and click the ✉️ icon on a neighbor's token to send a message.

<Callout type="info">
  Everyone sees an envelope being passed when you send a text message.
</Callout>

<figure>
  <Video src="/tutorials/whisper-at-night.mp4" />
</figure>

### Private chats

During the day, besides being able to whisper to your neighbors, you can invite anyone to a private voice chat:

- Press _Request Private Chat_ (or `[Enter]`)
- Click the 💬 icon on their token

They'll get an invite — no one else can join unless invited.

Leave a chat by:

- Pressing the exit icon in the _Voice_ panel
- Or pressing `[Backspace]`

<Callout type="info">
  Everyone can see who’s chatting with whom at all times.
</Callout>
