# Official scripts

In *Blood on the Clocktower*, a **script** is a curated set of characters used to build the game.

There are three official scripts. We play a wide variety of them, including unofficial ones. **Trouble Brewing** is the one that we'll try to play with new players.

When there's fewer players, we play [Teensyville](/rules/teensyville) games instead.
