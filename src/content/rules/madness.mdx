# Madness

There are characters, such as [Cerenovus](/scripts/sects-and-violets#cerenovus), or [Mutant](/scripts/sects-and-violets#mutant), that refer to _madness_.

If you are _mad_ about something, this means that you're trying to convince other players that something is true.

It's up to the Storyteller to assess whether the player affected by madness is acting as instructed, and whether they should suffer consequences (or a boon) based on that.

You're never forced to interact with madness, if you're so inclined, you can openly disobey it, and just receive an appropriate penalty _(Example: **Cerenovus** makes you mad as Clockmaker, you know there's a real Clockmaker and you don't want to create confusion. You openly say that you've been made mad and the Storyteller decides to execute you.)_
